## PPT Style Suggestion

- Use dark blue or white academic theme.
- Use no more than 5 bullet points per slide.
- Use charts and screenshots instead of long paragraphs.
- Keep the system boundary clear: offline prototype, not real-time platform.
- Put detailed Q and A in backup slides or prepare separately.
