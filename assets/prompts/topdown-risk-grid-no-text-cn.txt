Academic thesis defense PowerPoint illustration, 16:9 widescreen.

Create a pure top-down orthographic view of a dark city block grid map showing uneven spatial distribution of traffic collision risk.

Main subject:
- A square 10 by 10 grid system, exactly 100 grid cells.
- Not every cell is occupied.
- About 64 cells contain square blocks, irregularly distributed to simulate real urban irregular region coverage.
- The remaining cells are empty and show only the dark grid background.

Risk colors:
- Most occupied blocks are deep navy blue or dark charcoal gray, representing low-risk regions.
- Only 5 to 8 blocks use warm highlight colors.
- 2 to 3 highlighted blocks are deep red, representing high-risk hotspots.
- 3 to 5 highlighted blocks are orange, representing medium-high risk.
- Warm highlighted blocks must be scattered unevenly across the grid.
- Do not concentrate warm blocks in the same row, same column, or one corner.
- Do not make large areas red or orange.

Glow:
- Each warm highlighted block has a soft radial glow around it.
- The glow should spread gently across nearby 2 to 3 grid cells.
- The glow represents risk spreading to neighboring regions.
- Keep the glow controlled, soft, and academic, not flashy.

Road network:
- Very thin semi-transparent cyan straight lines connect some occupied blocks.
- The lines should form a sparse road network.
- Lines must be subtle and not dominate the image.

Strict visual requirements:
- Pure top-down orthographic view.
- No perspective tilt.
- No isometric view.
- The 10 by 10 grid must be a true square, not stretched.
- Deep dark background, almost black with a slight blue undertone.
- Clean flat visualization style.
- Restrained neon technology feeling.
- No text.
- No numbers.
- No labels.
- No legend.
- No coordinate axes.
- No icons.
- No cars.
- No people.
- No watermark.

Use case:
- Suitable for an academic thesis defense PowerPoint "Research Problem" slide.
