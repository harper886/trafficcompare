# 项目摘要与关键词

## 中文摘要

随着城市交通规模不断扩大，交通碰撞风险预测对提升道路安全管理能力具有重要意义。针对交通碰撞数据存在时序依赖、空间关联和类别不平衡等特点，本文设计并实现了一套基于 Attention-LSTM 的交通碰撞风险预测与可解释分析系统。系统以 NYC 和 Chicago 双城交通数据为研究对象，构建包含交通时序特征、事故标签、区域坐标映射以及道路、POI、历史记录等空间邻接关系的数据输入。模型部分采用 Attention-LSTM 结构对交通风险的动态变化进行建模，并结合平滑门和流式后处理机制增强预测结果稳定性。实验部分使用 AUC-PR、AUC-ROC、F1、Accuracy 和 Recall 等指标对模型进行评估，并与多种基线模型进行对比，同时通过消融实验验证关键模块的有效性。系统实现部分将模型预测结果、真实标签校验结果、实验指标和空间拓扑关系导出为前端 JSON 文件，并构建可视化大屏展示风险热力图、时间轴演化、TP/FP/FN 校验和局部空间解释。实验结果表明，本文方法在双城数据集上具有较好的风险识别能力，能够为交通碰撞预警和可视化分析提供参考。

## English Abstract

With the continuous growth of urban traffic systems, traffic collision risk prediction plays an important role in improving road safety management. Considering the temporal dependency, spatial correlation and class imbalance in traffic collision data, this project designs and implements an Attention-LSTM based traffic collision risk forecasting and explainable visualization system. The system uses NYC and Chicago traffic datasets, including traffic time-series features, crash labels, region mappings, and spatial adjacency relations derived from road, POI and historical records. The proposed model adopts an Attention-LSTM structure to capture dynamic traffic risk patterns, and introduces smoothing and streaming post-processing mechanisms to improve output stability. Experiments are conducted with multiple metrics including AUC-PR, AUC-ROC, F1, Accuracy and Recall, and the proposed method is compared with several baseline models. Ablation studies are also performed to validate the effectiveness of key modules. For system implementation, prediction results, label verification results, evaluation metrics and topology information are exported into frontend JSON files and visualized in an interactive dashboard. The dashboard presents risk heatmaps, timeline evolution, TP/FP/FN verification and local spatial explanations. Experimental results show that the proposed method achieves effective risk identification on both city datasets and provides useful support for traffic collision warning and visual analysis.
